Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Goolz ("the app") is developed by MTRSoft. Your privacy matters; this policy explains what the app does with your data.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. The developer has no servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

The goals and progress entries you create are stored locally on your device.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app may sync your goals across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit. This data is stored in your private iCloud account, is managed by Apple, and is not accessible to the developer.

In-app purchases

The app offers an optional one-time "Premium" purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time using the in-app "Delete all data" option or by deleting the app. iCloud data can be managed in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
